Privacy Policy for PhishGuard

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

PhishGuard ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles information.

1. Data Collection and Usage

  • Local Processing Only: PhishGuard operates 100% locally within your web browser. All URL scanning and phishing detection are performed on your device.
  • No Data Collection: We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, email contents, browsing history, or sensitive information to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

PhishGuard does not use any third-party analytics, tracking tools, or external APIs that collect user data.

3. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via GitHub.

PhishGuard 隱私權政策（中文版）

生效日期： 2026年5月1日

PhishGuard（以下簡稱「我們」）極度重視您的隱私權。本隱私權政策說明我們的 Chrome 擴充功能如何處理相關資訊。

1. 數據收集與使用

  • 僅限本地運算： PhishGuard 100% 在您的網頁瀏覽器本地端運行。所有網址掃描與釣魚檢測均於您的裝置上執行。

2. 第三方服務

PhishGuard 未使用任何會收集用戶數據的第三方分析工具、追蹤器或外部 API。

3. 隱私權政策之變更

我們可能會不時更新隱私權政策。任何變更都將發布在此頁面上。
